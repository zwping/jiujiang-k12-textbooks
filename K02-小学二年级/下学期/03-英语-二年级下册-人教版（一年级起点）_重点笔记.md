# 二年级下册英语·重点笔记

> 教材：人教版英语（一年级起点）二年级下册。\

## 二、目录与章节重点

### 单元重点

#### Unit 1 Playtime：活动和能力

- **词汇**：`play football` 踢足球、`fly a kite` 放风筝、`ride a bike` 骑自行车、`make a model plane` 制作模型飞机、`swim` 游泳、`make a snowman` 堆雪人。
- **核心句型**：`Can you play football? — Yes, I can. / No, I can't.`
- **表达能力**：`I can swim.`；`I can't fly a kite.`
- **重点**：`Can you ...?` 后接动词原形；肯定回答 `Yes, I can.`，否定回答 `No, I can't.`。

#### Unit 2 Weather：天气

- **词汇**：`sunny` 晴朗的、`cloudy` 多云的、`rainy` 下雨的、`snowy` 下雪的、`windy` 有风的、`hot` 热的、`cold` 冷的。
- **核心句型**：`What's the weather like today? — It's sunny.`
- **表达感受**：`It's cold today.` / `It's hot today.`
- **练习**：每天观察天气，用日期+天气说一句完整英语。

#### Unit 3 Seasons：季节

- **词汇**：`spring` 春天、`summer` 夏天、`autumn` 秋天、`winter` 冬天；`warm` 暖和的、`hot` 热的、`cool` 凉爽的、`cold` 寒冷的。
- **核心句型**：`What season is it? — It's spring.`\
  `What's spring like? — It's warm.`
- **表达喜好**：`I like summer.`；能把季节与天气、活动联系起来。

#### Unit 4 Time：时间

- **词汇**：`o'clock` ……点钟、`get up` 起床、`go to school` 上学、`go home` 回家、`go to bed` 上床睡觉。
- **核心句型**：`What time is it? — It's seven o'clock.`
- **时间表达**：本册先掌握整点；数字后加 `o'clock`，如 `eight o'clock`。
- **情境**：把时间和日常活动配对：`I get up at seven o'clock.`

#### Unit 5 My Day：一天作息

- **词汇/短语**：`in the morning` 在早上、`at noon` 在中午、`in the afternoon` 在下午、`in the evening` 在晚上；`have breakfast/lunch/dinner`、`do homework`、`watch TV`。
- **核心句型**：`When do you get up? — I get up at seven.`\
  `What do you do in the evening? — I do my homework.`
- **重点**：按照 morning→noon→afternoon→evening 的顺序说一天活动。

#### Unit 6 My Week：星期和一周安排

- **词汇**：`Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday`；`week` 星期/一周、`today` 今天。
- **核心句型**：`What day is it today? — It's Monday.`\
  `What do you do on Sunday? — I play football.`
- **重点**：星期前常用 `on`，如 `on Monday`；注意 `Thursday`、`Wednesday` 的拼写和发音。

## 三、必须背熟的原文短句

```text
Can you [play football]? — Yes, I can. / No, I can't.
What's the weather like today? — It's [sunny].
What season is it? — It's [spring].
What's spring like? — It's [warm].
What time is it? — It's [seven] o'clock.
When do you get up? — I get up at [seven].
What day is it today? — It's [Monday].
What do you do on [Sunday]? — I [play football].
```

## 四、易错点与练习方法

1. `can` 后面的动作不加 `to`，说 `I can swim`，不说 `I can to swim`。
2. 天气问句固定为 `What's the weather like today?`；回答用 `It's + 天气词`。
3. 时间整点用 `数字 + o'clock`；星期活动用 `on + 星期`。
4. 每天完成“一张生活卡”：画出时间、天气、活动，再用本册句型说出来。
