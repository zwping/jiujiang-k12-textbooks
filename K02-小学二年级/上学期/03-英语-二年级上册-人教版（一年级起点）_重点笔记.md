## 二、目录与章节重点

### 单元重点

#### Unit 1 My Family：家庭成员

- **词汇**：`father` 爸爸、`mother` 妈妈、`brother` 兄弟、`sister` 姐妹、`grandfather` 祖父/外祖父、`grandmother` 祖母/外祖母。
- **核心句型**：`Who's he? — He is my father.`\
  `Who's she? — She is my mother.`
- **代词**：男性用 `he`，女性用 `she`；问“谁”用 `who`。
- **扩展表达**：`This is my family.`；`I love my family.`

#### Unit 2 Boys and Girls：人物身份和姓名

- **词汇**：`classmate` 同班同学、`friend` 朋友、`girl` 女孩、`boy` 男孩、`woman` 女人、`man` 男人、`name` 名字。
- **核心句型**：`What's his name? — His name is Panpan.`\
  `What's her name? — Her name is Lily.`
- **重点**：男孩/男性配 `his`、`he`；女孩/女性配 `her`、`she`。
- **交际**：介绍同学时可说 `He is my friend.` / `She is my classmate.`。

#### Unit 3 My Friends：外貌

- **词汇**：`big` 大的、`tall` 高的、`pretty` 漂亮的、`thin` 瘦的、`short` 矮的、`handsome` 英俊的、`new` 新的。
- **核心句型**：`What does he look like? — He is tall.`\
  `What does she look like? — She is pretty.`
- **句型整体记**：`What does ... look like?` 是询问外貌的固定表达，不逐词翻译成“他看起来做什么”。
- **描述顺序**：人物 + `is` + 外貌形容词，如 `He is big and tall.`。

#### Unit 4 In the Community：社区地点

- **词汇**：`bookshop` 书店、`zoo` 动物园、`school` 学校、`supermarket` 超市、`park` 公园、`hospital` 医院。
- **核心句型**：`Where are you going? — I'm going to the zoo.`
- **结构**：`go to + 地点` 表示“去某地”；地点前通常使用 `the`，如 `the park`、`the zoo`。
- **情境练习**：看到社区地图，先问目的地，再用 `I'm going to ...` 回答。

#### Unit 5 In the Park：景物、数量和位置

- **词汇**：`grass` 草、`tree` 树、`flower` 花、`boat` 小船、`lake` 湖、`hill` 小山。
- **核心句型**：`There is a boat on the lake.` —— 湖上有一条船。\
  `There are trees in the park.` —— 公园里有树。
- **单复数**：一个用 `There is a ...`；多个用 `There are ...`。
- **方位**：继续使用 `in/on/under` 等位置词描述景物。

#### Unit 6 Happy Holidays：节日、礼物与感谢

- **词汇**：`Christmas` 圣诞节、`Father Christmas` 圣诞老人、`Christmas tree` 圣诞树、`card` 贺卡、`present` 礼物、`New Year` 新年、`merry` 愉快的、`happy` 快乐的。
- **核心句型**：`Merry Christmas!`；`Happy New Year!`\
  `Here is a present for you.` —— 这是给你的礼物。\
  `Thank you.` —— 谢谢。
- **礼貌闭环**：收到礼物说 `Thank you.`；祝福对方可回应 `Merry Christmas to you!`。

## 三、必须背熟的原文短句

```text
Who's he? — He is my father.
Who's she? — She is my mother.
What's his name? — His name is [Panpan].
What's her name? — Her name is [Lily].
What does he look like? — He is [tall].
Where are you going? — I'm going to the [zoo].
There is a [boat] on the [lake].
There are [trees] in the [park].
Here is a present for you. — Thank you.
Merry Christmas! / Happy New Year!
```

## 四、易错点

1. `he/his` 与 `she/her` 要成组记忆：`he—his`，`she—her`。
2. `Who's` 是 `Who is` 的缩写；`What's` 是 `What is` 的缩写；朗读时不能漏掉缩写中的音。
3. `There is` 后接一个事物，`There are` 后接多个事物。
4. `Where are you going?` 问的是“要去哪里”，回答用 `I'm going to ...`，不是 `I am ...`。
