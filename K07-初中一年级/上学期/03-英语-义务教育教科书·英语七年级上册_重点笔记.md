## 二、目录与章节重点

### Starter Units

#### Starter Unit 1 Good morning!

**词汇**：`I`我、`you`你、`am`是、`are`是、`fine`好的、`OK`好、`hello/hi`你好、`thanks`谢谢；`good morning`早上好、`good afternoon`下午好、`good evening`晚上好。

**句型**：

```text
Good morning, Alice!
Good afternoon, Eric!
Good evening, Bob!
Hello! / Hi!
How are you? I'm fine, thanks. / I'm OK.
```

#### Starter Unit 2 What's this in English?

**词汇**：`map`地图、`key`钥匙、`jacket`夹克、`cup`杯子、`ruler`尺子、`pen`钢笔、`orange`橙子/橙色。

**句型**：

```text
What's this in English? It's an orange.
What's that in English? It's a jacket.
Spell it, please. O-R-A-N-G-E.
```

`this`指近处，`that`指远处；`orange`以元音音素开头，用`an orange`，`a jacket`用`a`。

#### Starter Unit 3 What color is it?

**颜色**：`red`红、`yellow`黄、`green`绿、`blue`蓝、`black`黑、`white`白、`purple`紫、`brown`棕。

**句型**：`What's this/that? It's V.`；`What color is it? It's red.`

### Unit 1 My name's Gina

#### 词汇与功能

`name`名字、`telephone/phone number`电话号码、`first name`名、`last name`姓、`middle school`中学、`your`你的、`his`他的、`her`她的、`he`他、`she`她、`yes/no`是/不是。

#### 必背句型

```text
What's your name? My name's Gina. / I'm Gina.
Hello, Alan. I'm Ms. Brown.
Nice to meet you. Nice to meet you, too.
Are you Helen? No, I'm not. I'm Gina.
What's your telephone number? It's 281-9176.
```

#### 语法

- `I am`缩写为`I'm`；`what is`缩写为`what's`；`name is`缩写为`name's`。
- be动词：`I am`，`you/we/they are`，`he/she/it is`。
- 一般疑问句：`Are you ...?`，肯定回答`Yes, I am.`，否定回答`No, I'm not.`。
- 物主代词：`my`我的、`your`你的、`his`他的、`her`她的，后面接名词。

### Unit 2 This is my sister

#### 词汇

`family`家庭；`mother/mom`母亲；`father/dad`父亲；`parents`父母；`grandmother/grandma`祖母；`grandfather/grandpa`祖父；`brother`兄弟；`sister`姐妹；`cousin`堂/表兄弟姐妹；`aunt`姑/姨；`uncle`叔/舅；`son`儿子；`daughter`女儿；`friend`朋友。

#### 必背句型

```text
Who's she? She's my sister.
Who's he? He's my brother.
This/That is my friend Jane.
These/Those are my brothers.
Who are they? They're my grandparents.
```

#### 语法

- `this`这个、`that`那个；`these`这些、`those`那些。
- `this/that`后接单数名词，`these/those`后接复数名词。
- `he`指男性，`she`指女性，`they`指复数人或物。
- 一般名词变复数通常加`s`：`brother-brothers`；以特定字母结尾的名词按规则加`es`。

### Unit 3 Is this your pencil?

#### 词汇

`pencil`铅笔、`pencil box`铅笔盒、`schoolbag`书包、`book`书、`eraser`橡皮、`notebook`笔记本、`dictionary`词典、`baseball`棒球、`computer game`电脑游戏、`ID card`身份证、`watch`手表、`ring`戒指。

#### 必背句型

```text
Is this your pencil? Yes, it is. It's mine.
Is that your schoolbag? No, it isn't. It's his.
Are these your books? No, they aren't. They're hers.
How do you spell it? P-E-N-C-I-L.
Thank you for your help. You're welcome.
```

#### 语法

- 物主代词：`mine`我的、`yours`你的、`his`他的、`hers`她的，可以独立作表语，后面不再接名词。
- `Is this ...?`回答`Yes, it is.` / `No, it isn't.`；`Are these ...?`回答`Yes, they are.` / `No, they aren't.`。
- `your pencil`是“你的铅笔”，`mine`是“我的（铅笔）”，不能说`mine pencil`。

### Unit 4 Where's my schoolbag?

#### 词汇

`room`房间、`table`桌子、`bed`床、`sofa`沙发、`chair`椅子、`desk`书桌、`radio`收音机、`clock`时钟、`tape player`录音机、`model plane`飞机模型、`under`在……下面、`where`在哪里。

#### 必背句型

```text
Where's my schoolbag? It's under the table.
Where's his pencil box? It's in his schoolbag.
Where are my books? They're on the sofa.
The computer game is under the bed.
I have a clock, and my sister has a radio.
```

#### 语法

- `where's`=`where is`，询问单数物品位置；`where are`询问复数物品位置。
- `in`在里面，`on`在上面，`under`在下面。
- 单数回答`It's ...`，复数回答`They're ...`。
- `and`连接两个并列的词、短语或句子。

### Unit 5 Do you have a soccer ball?

#### 词汇

`tennis ball`网球、`ping-pong`乒乓球、`soccer`足球、`volleyball`排球、`basketball`篮球、`bat`球拍、`TV`电视、`interesting`有趣的、`boring`无聊的、`fun`有趣的、`difficult`困难的、`relaxing`轻松的、`easy`容易的。

#### 必背句型

```text
Do you have a ping-pong bat? Yes, I do.
No, I don't. I have a ping-pong ball.
Does she have a tennis ball? No, she doesn't.
She has a baseball.
Let's play basketball. That sounds good.
I don't have a soccer ball, but my brother does.
```

#### 语法

- `have`表示“有”；主语是第三人称单数时用`has`。
- 一般疑问句：`Do you have ...?`；第三人称单数：`Does he/she have ...?`。
- `does`出现时，后面的动词用原形：`Does she have ...?`，不是`has`。
- 否定：`I don't have ...`；`He/She doesn't have ...`。
- `but`表示转折，连接两个意思相反或不同的分句。

### Unit 6 Do you like bananas?

#### 词汇

`hamburger`汉堡包、`tomato`西红柿、`ice-cream`冰激凌、`salad`沙拉、`banana`香蕉、`strawberry`草莓、`pear`梨、`milk`牛奶、`bread`面包、`chicken`鸡肉、`vegetable`蔬菜、`fruit`水果、`apple`苹果、`rice`米饭、`egg`鸡蛋、`carrot`胡萝卜、`breakfast`早餐、`lunch`午餐、`dinner`晚餐。

#### 必背句型

```text
Do you like salad? Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
Does he like ice-cream? Yes, he does.
He likes ice-cream. He doesn't like vegetables.
I like fruit, but I don't like vegetables.
What about you? I like apples.
Let's have strawberries. Sounds good.
```

#### 语法

- `like`后接可数名词时，通常用复数表示一类：`I like bananas.`。
- `he/she/it likes`，否定为`he/she/it doesn't like`。
- `Do you like ...?`回答`Yes, I do.` / `No, I don't.`；`Does he like ...?`回答`Yes, he does.` / `No, he doesn't.`。
- 不可数名词通常没有复数形式：`milk`、`rice`、`bread`。

### Unit 7 How much are these socks?

#### 词汇

`clothes`衣服、`socks`袜子、`T-shirt`T恤衫、`shorts`短裤、`sweater`毛衣、`trousers`裤子、`shoes`鞋、`skirt`裙子、`dollar`美元、`big`大、`small`小、`short`短、`long`长、`pair`一双/一条。

#### 必背句型

```text
How much is the hat? It's five dollars.
How much is this/that T-shirt? It's seven dollars.
How much are these/those socks? They're two dollars.
Can I help you? Yes, please.
I'll take it/them. Here you are.
```

#### 语法

- 单数：`How much is this/that ...?`，回答`It's ...`。
- 复数：`How much are these/those ...?`，回答`They're ...`。
- `this/these`指近处，`that/those`指远处；单数配`is`，复数配`are`。
- 一双或一条用`a pair of`，如`a pair of socks`。

### Unit 8 When is your birthday?

#### 词汇

月份：`January February March April May June July August September October November December`。

活动：`English test`英语测试、`school trip`学校旅行、`birthday party`生日聚会、`basketball game`篮球赛、`art festival`艺术节、`Sports Day`体育节、`English Day`英语节、`School Day`学校开放日、`book sale`售书活动。

#### 日期与序数词

`first`1st、`second`2nd、`third`3rd、`fifth`5th、`eighth`8th、`ninth`9th、`twelfth`12th、`twentieth`20th、`thirtieth`30th；其余常见日期按序数词规则变化。

#### 必背句型

```text
When is your birthday? My birthday is on May 2nd.
When is her birthday? It's in August.
When is Sally's birthday party? It's on October 5th.
When is the school trip? It's on September 21st.
```

#### 语法

- 具体某一天用`on`：`on May 2nd`；月份前用`in`：`in August`。
- `Sally's birthday`中的`'s`表示“萨莉的”。
- 日期表达中月份首字母大写，序数词表示“第几日”。

### Unit 9 My favorite subject is science

#### 词汇

`subject`科目、`P.E.`体育、`science`科学、`music`音乐、`math`数学、`geography`地理、`Chinese`语文、`history`历史、`favorite`最喜欢的、`useful`有用的、`cool`酷的、`free`空闲的、`lesson`课、`why`为什么、`because`因为。

星期：`Monday Tuesday Wednesday Thursday Friday Saturday Sunday`。

#### 必背句型

```text
What's your favorite subject? My favorite subject is science.
Why do you like P.E.? Because it's fun.
Who is your P.E. teacher? Mr. Hu.
When is your geography class? It's on Friday.
What's David's favorite subject? His favorite subject is ...
```

#### 语法

- `What`问什么，`Why`问原因，`Who`问人，`When`问时间。
- `Why ...?`回答常用`Because ...`，不能只回答`Because`。
- 星期和科目名称首字母通常大写；`P.E.`带标点。
- `from ... to ...`表示从……到……，用于课程时间或地点范围。

## 十三、易错点清单

- `Does he have ...?`中用`have`，不能写`has`；肯定句才是`He has ...`。
- `He likes ...`第三人称单数动词加`s`；`He doesn't like ...`中恢复原形。
- `Where are my books? They're ...`复数不能回答`It's`。
- `How much is ...?`对应单数，`How much are ...?`对应复数。
- `on May 2nd`是具体日期，`in August`是月份。
- `my/your/his/her`后面接名词；`mine/yours/his/hers`可以独立使用。
- `a/an`根据发音选择，不是单纯看字母；`an orange`、`a university`。
